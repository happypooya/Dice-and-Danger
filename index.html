📜 Dungeons, Dice & Danger - Quick Rules

  • Objective: Players use dice to travel through Dungeons, fight Monsters, and collect Treasure to earn the highest total Victory points.
  • Setup: Each player takes an Adventure sheet and pencil. Players choose one of 4 Dungeon maps.
  • Dice Pool: Uses 5 dice total: 4 White dice and 1 Black die.
  • Rolling: Active player rolls all 5 dice into the center for everyone to see.
  • Forming Pairs: Every player builds 2 pairs of dice combinations and calculates the sum of each pair.
  • Marking Spaces: Sum your 2 dice pairs and cross off 2 corresponding numbered spaces on your sheet per round.
  • Adjacency Rule: Subsequent spaces can only be marked if they are connected by a valid path to a previously visited space.
