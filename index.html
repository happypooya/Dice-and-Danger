📜 Dungeons, Dice & Danger - Quick Rules

  • Objective: Players use dice to travel through Dungeons, fight Monsters, and collect Treasure to earn the highest total Victory points.
  • Setup: Each player takes an Adventure sheet and pencil. Players choose one of 4 Dungeon maps (Annoyed Animals, Clumsy Cultists, Puzzled Pyramid, or Defiant Dinosaurs).
  • Dice Pool: Uses 5 dice total: 4 White dice and 1 Black die.
  • Rolling: Active player rolls all 5 dice into the center for everyone to see.
  • Forming Pairs: Every player builds 2 pairs of dice combinations and calculates the sum of each pair.
  • Active Player: May freely substitute 1 White die with the Black die in 1 of their pairs to create combinations.
  • Passive Players: Must build pairs using only the 4 White dice. To substitute the Black die, a passive player must cross off 1 of their 3 starting Black die symbols on their board.
  • Marking Spaces: Sum your 2 dice pairs and cross off 2 corresponding numbered spaces on your sheet per round.
  • Adjacency Rule: Enter from any Start space (do not need to connect). Subsequent spaces can only be marked if they are adjacent to a previously visited space.
  • Life Penalty: If you cannot validly use a dice pair, lose 1 Life point from your tracker for each unused pair.
  • Dealing Damage: Match a dice pair to an available room number to check off a Monster's Life box.
  • Black numbers inside the room can be used immediately. Other numbers unlock once the gray space adjacent to the room is visited.
  • Defeating a Monster: Defeated once all Life boxes are crossed off.
  • Gem Rewards: First player to defeat a Monster earns the 1st Reward; players who defeat it in subsequent rounds get the Sub Reward.
  • Group Attack: When any player first defeats a Monster, all other players immediately deal 1 free Damage to that Monster.
  • Reaching a Treasure Chest space unlocks 1 permanent upgrade:
    • Extra Life: Gain 3 additional Life points + 1 Gem.
    • The Torch: Immediately mark any space adjacent to a visited space (can damage a monster).
    • Extra Black Dice: Gain 3 additional passive uses of the Black die.
  • Trigger: Game ends at the end of the round in which any player defeats their last Monster.
  • Boss Bonus: Players who have not defeated their boss Monster gain 3 Victory points for every 3 Damage dealt to it.
  • Victory: Total all Victory points (including Gold and Gems) and subtract lost Life points. Highest final score wins!
